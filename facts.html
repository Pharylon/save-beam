Financial Misalignment

The district claims closing W.B. Beam will save $288,342. However, a deeper audit shows the actual net savings is much lower.

  • Suspicious Savings Claims: SRO grants and Title 1 allocations will simply transfer with the students, shrinking net savings to an estimated $90,000 - $160,000.
  • No Baseline Budget Tracking: Public records show Gaston County Schools does not track individual operating budgets per school, making it impossible to calculate exact savings.

School Overcrowding

W.B. Beam Intermediate currently operates at a highly stable and efficient 87% utilization rate.

  • Cherryville Elementary Impact: Moving Beam's students to Cherryville Elementary will immediately force utilization there to jump to 97%.
  • Strained Classrooms: Overcrowded environments degrade student safety, reduce personal instruction, and put excess stress on teachers.

Cherryville's Rapid Growth

Closing a local school building runs contrary to Cherryville's current upward residential expansion trend.

Educational Impact

Transitioning fifth-grade elementary students into John Chavis Middle School is developmentally disruptive.

  • Developmental Risks: Fifth graders thrive in intermediate environments. Forcing 10-year-olds into middle school structures creates avoidable peer pressure and safety issues.
